MPCLock — Bank of England MPC Hawkishness

An LLM pairwise tournament scoring UK monetary-policy communication on a hawkish–dovish spectrum: MPC members' monetary-policy speeches alongside the Committee's own minutes, Monetary Policy Reports (full text, annexes included) and press conferences. 0 = neutral; positive = more hawkish (tighter), negative = more dovish. Method adapted from FedLock. Scores reflect LLM judgment, not official characterizations.

Add a press interview

Paste a link to an interview an MPC member gave to the press (newspaper, broadcaster, podcast) and — if the article is paywalled — the transcript or article text, then press Add to corpus. Leave the member and date on auto and they are read from the article. The intake job checks it against everything already held (the same link, the same interview reported by another outlet, or an article that only reports a speech), classifies it for monetary-policy relevance, adds it if relevant and codes the vote it signals. The result appears here in about a minute; the forecast and the site refresh a few minutes later.

Access token

Adding to the corpus needs a GitHub token that may start this site's intake job. It is stored only in this browser (never in the site's code) and sent only to api.github.com. Without one you can still check for duplicates, or submit by GitHub issue.
