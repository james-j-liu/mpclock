MPCLock — Bank of England MPC Hawkishness

An LLM pairwise tournament scoring UK monetary-policy communication on a hawkish–dovish spectrum: MPC members' monetary-policy speeches alongside the Committee's own minutes, Monetary Policy Reports (full text, annexes included) and press conferences. 0 = neutral; positive = more hawkish (tighter), negative = more dovish. Method adapted from FedLock. Scores reflect LLM judgment, not official characterizations.

Add a press interview

Paste a link to an interview an MPC member gave to the press (newspaper, broadcaster, podcast), and — if the article is paywalled — the transcript or article text. Submitting opens a pre-filled GitHub issue; leave the member and date on auto and they are read from the article. When you file it, the intake job checks it against everything already held (the same link, the same interview reported by another outlet, or an article that only reports a speech), classifies it for monetary-policy relevance, adds it to the corpus if relevant and codes the vote it signals. The result is posted on the issue, and the forecast refreshes.
