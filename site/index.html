MPCLock — Bank of England MPC Hawkishness

An LLM pairwise tournament scoring UK monetary-policy communication on a hawkish–dovish spectrum: MPC members' monetary-policy speeches alongside the Committee's own minutes, Monetary Policy Reports (full text, annexes included) and press conferences. 0 = neutral; positive = more hawkish (tighter), negative = more dovish. Method adapted from FedLock. Scores reflect LLM judgment, not official characterizations.
